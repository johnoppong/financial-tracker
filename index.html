Settings & Google Sheets Sync Setup

Provide your Google Apps Script URL to automatically sync orders & cashbook entries to Google Sheets.

Google Apps Script Setup Guide

  1. Open your Google Sheet (e.g. Financial tracker 1.xlsx).
  2. Go to Extensions > Apps Script in the Google Sheets top menu.
  3. Paste the `doPost` boilerplate script into `Code.gs`.
  4. Click Deploy > New Deployment, select Web app, set access to Anyone, and click Deploy.
  5. Copy the generated Web App URL and paste it into the field above!
